Who We Are

A Family United By Care And Responsibility.

Mia Moja Old Clan Welfare Group was established in 1989.

Our identity is built around the belief that we are one clan and one family, sharing a future and caring for one another.

Our purpose is to promote family welfare, support members and improve the well-being of our community.
